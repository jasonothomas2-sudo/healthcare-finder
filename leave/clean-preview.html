How it's calculated
  • Daily earning: yearly days ÷ 365 for each day since leave was last taken. The leave year is not the calendar year.
  • Sick leave: only excess sick days come out of vacation earned. Free allowance is 14 days, or 10 for non-uniform staff on 15 days a year who joined after 2002.
  • Limit: up to 3 years of entitlement can be held. Extra days are lost.
  • Earned Beyond Maximum: up to 2 more years (5 total).
